1. Introduction

Smart Financial Calculator respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the app's calculators and features.

By using the app, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

2. Information and Calculator Data

Financial values and calculator inputs are processed locally on your device. The app does not transmit your calculator inputs to us or to the currency-rate provider.

The app does not provide Calculation History or Cloud Backup and Restore features.

The current version does not include third-party advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs.

3. Currency Converter and Third-Party Service

The Currency Converter uses ExchangeRate-API to retrieve exchange rates. The app provides five free Currency Converter uses per device. After the five free uses, you need to add your own ExchangeRate-API key to continue using the converter.

The five-use allowance is stored locally on your device. Clearing app data or reinstalling the app may reset this local counter. This allowance is separate from the API provider's own monthly request quota.

Without a personal API key, the app may use the ExchangeRate-API Open Access endpoint:

https://open.er-api.com

If you add your own API key, the app stores it in the private SharedPreferences storage of your Android app. The key is not encrypted by the app. It is sent to ExchangeRate-API over HTTPS using an Authorization: Bearer header when making authenticated requests.

For authenticated requests, the API key and requested currency information are sent to ExchangeRate-API. The provider may also receive your IP address and technical request information.

ExchangeRate-API may apply its own monthly usage limits or account restrictions. Reaching the provider's quota may prevent further API requests even if the app's five-free-use allowance has been passed or a personal API key has been added.

Exchange rates used by the Currency Converter are cached locally on your device for up to 24 hours. Cached rates may be used when an internet connection is unavailable. Rates may therefore not always be real-time.

4. Dashboard Currency Rate

The dashboard may display an USD-to-INR exchange rate retrieved from ExchangeRate-API. This dashboard rate uses a separate local cache and is independent of the Currency Converter's five-free-use counter.

The dashboard rate is cached for up to 24 hours. Under normal operation, the app attempts to refresh this rate no more than once within a 24-hour period. The displayed rate may be up to 24 hours old and should not be considered a guaranteed live rate.

If an API key is configured, the dashboard may use it for the rate request. Otherwise, it may use the Open Access service where available.

5. Locally Stored Preferences

The app stores selected currency preferences, theme settings, API key (if provided), local usage counter and cached exchange rates locally on your device.

This locally stored information is used to provide app features and preferences.

You are responsible for keeping your device secure and protecting access to it.
